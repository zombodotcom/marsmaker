What the converter fixed

USGS stores these DTMs in a global map projection where east–west distances are only true at the equator. At 47°S, a naive import stretches every dune 47% too wide. The converter reprojects each site onto a local true-scale grid.

The photo for this site also carried a georeference 8.26 km off from its own terrain. The converter caught that automatically by matching the image against the terrain's slopes.
